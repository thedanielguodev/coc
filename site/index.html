Each point is one CoC in one year: acres burned within that CoC's boundary (x) vs. its homeless count (y). Points at x = 0 are CoC-years with no major (≥1,000-acre) fire on record. Line is the least-squares fit. Large urban CoCs dominate homeless totals but rarely burn heavily, while large rural CoCs burn heavily but have small populations — this population/geography mismatch pulls the raw correlation toward zero independent of any real relationship, so treat r as a starting point, not a verdict.
